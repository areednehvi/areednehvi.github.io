Shadow Detective Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

In short: Shadow Detective does not collect, store or share any personal information. It has no ads, no accounts, no in-app purchases and works fully offline.

Shadow Detective is a picture puzzle game for all ages, including children, made by BeyondAI (Arif Nazir). This policy explains how the app handles information.

Information we collect

None. Shadow Detective does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal information. There are no accounts, logins or sign-ups, and the app does not ask for names, email addresses, photos, location or contacts.

Internet, ads and analytics

Shadow Detective works fully offline and does not connect to the internet. It contains no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party services that collect data. There are no in-app purchases: the coins in the game are earned only by playing and cannot be bought. There are no links that leave the app.

Data stored on the device

Stars, scores, coins, the detective's outfit and the app's settings are stored only on the device. They are never sent anywhere. Uninstalling the app, or choosing "Start over" in its settings, deletes them.

Permissions

Shadow Detective does not ask for any permissions. It does not use the camera, microphone, contacts, location or photo library.

Children's privacy

Shadow Detective is suitable for children and follows the Google Play Families Policy and the App Store guidelines for the Kids category. Because no personal information is collected, no information from children is collected.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer, BeyondAI (Arif Nazir), using the contact email shown on Shadow Detective's page in Google Play or the App Store.
